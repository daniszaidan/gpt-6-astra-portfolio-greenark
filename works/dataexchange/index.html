01 / Overview

Making complex data feel clear.

Data Exchange brings data management, insights, and infographics together across Telkom’s business verticals. I first joined as a frontend developer, then contributed to the redesign and redevelopment alongside designers, backend engineers, and data analysts.

02 / The approach

Give complex information a clearer hierarchy. The redesign revisits the entry point, data categories, search, catalogue details, and editorial data stories to support evolving business needs.

01 /

A clearer first impression.

The landing page introduces the platform’s purpose and guides visitors into the data catalogue. The before-and-after views document the visual and structural changes.

02 /

From searching to understanding.

Reworked search and catalogue details help users move from a broad question to relevant information. Consistent components hold the different content types together.

03 /

Data with a story to tell.

The Kisah Data experience gives insight-led content its own rhythm, with an overview, listing, and detailed reading experience.
