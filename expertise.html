A few good questions

Before we begin.

Can you help with both design and development?+

Yes. I work across UI/UX design and frontend development, which helps keep the experience consistent from early concepts to implementation. We can also focus on one part of your project.

What does a typical collaboration look like?+

We start by understanding the problem, audience, and scope. From there, we agree on a direction, work in visible stages, and make room for feedback throughout.

Can you join an existing team or product?+

Absolutely. My experience includes collaborating with UI/UX designers, backend engineers, data analysts, and product owners on existing products as well as new interfaces.

How do we work out timing and cost?+

Both depend on scope, complexity, and the materials already available. Send a short brief with your goals and any target dates, and we can work out a realistic next step.
