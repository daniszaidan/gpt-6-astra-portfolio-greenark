01 / Overview

A little closer to your next favourite film.

WatchLur brings movies, series, people, and trailers into one cinematic browsing experience. Originally a collaboration with Arkan for Telkom DEVORIA, it became a personal space to explore a new visual direction and modern frontend development.

02 / The approach

Make discovery feel as engaging as the films themselves. Large imagery sets the mood, while focused navigation, useful filters, and clear information help people find their next watch.

01 /

Discovery, without the dead ends.

Browse now-playing titles, popular picks, and highly rated films and series. Advanced search separates movies, TV, and people, with filters to refine the results.

02 /

The whole story, in one place.

Movie details bring together the synopsis, genres, production information, cast, and crew. Image and video galleries make room to explore beyond the poster.

03 /

Care in the in-between moments.

Skeleton states, query caching, infinite scroll, responsive layouts, and preview cards keep the experience continuous. Watch-provider links and embedded trailers help turn discovery into a decision.
