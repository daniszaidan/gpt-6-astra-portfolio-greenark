01 / Overview

Less admin. More room to grow.

Alrizan is a product-management dashboard with real-time data and social-sharing tools. It brings product information, image galleries, brands, and marketplace links into a single responsive workspace.

02 / The approach

Make repetitive work easier to manage. Clear completion states surface missing details, image compression helps keep uploads manageable, and reusable sharing tools connect product data with social channels.

01 /

A product’s details, together.

Add and edit products with galleries, sizes, colours, brands, and posting status. Completion indicators make unfinished entries easier to spot.

02 /

A shorter route to sharing.

Generated descriptions and hashtags, copyable product information, and batch image downloads support a practical publishing workflow.

03 /

An organised foundation.

Google authentication, multi-level user management, and searchable brand records keep everyday administration accessible and structured.
